About Compressify

Who We Are

Compressify is a file compression platform designed to help users reduce the size of supported images and PDF documents.


Our Mission

Our mission is to provide an easy-to-use tool that helps users prepare smaller files for sharing, storage, and other digital purposes.


What We Offer

Our platform provides image and PDF compression functionality through a simple website interface. Compression results depend on the file type, settings, and processing method.
